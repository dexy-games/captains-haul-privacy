### 2. Information We Collect
We collect minimal data to provide gameplay and leaderboard services:

* **Display Name:** The name you choose for the global leaderboards.

### 5. Data Retention
* **Game Data:** We retain leaderboard and progress data as long as your account is active. Accounts inactive for more than 24 months may be deleted.
* **Financial Records:** Data related to in-app purchases is retained for 6 years to comply with UK tax (HMRC) requirements.

### 6. Your Rights (UK & GDPR)
Under the UK GDPR, you have the following rights:
* **Access:** Request a copy of the data we hold about your Player ID.
* **Erasure:** Request that we delete your account and leaderboard data.
* **Rectification:** Update your leaderboard display name.

To exercise these rights, please visit our [Data Request Page](https://www.dexygames.com/data-request) or email **info@dexygames.com**. We respond to all valid requests within 30 days.

Captain's Haul is designed for a general audience. We comply with the **UK Children’s Code (AADC)** and do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.
