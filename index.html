1. Introduction

This policy explains how DEXY GAMES LTD ("we", "us") handles your data in Captain's Haul. We act as a "Data Controller" under the UK GDPR.

2. Information We Collect

  • Information You Provide: Your chosen Display Name and any email address/Player ID submitted via our support or data request forms.
  • Automated Collection: A unique Player ID generated by the app, IP address, device model, and OS version.
  • Gameplay Data: High scores, leaderboard ranking, and virtual currency (Doubloon) balances.
  • In-App Purchase Data: We receive transaction receipts from Google Play to validate and deliver your purchases.

3. Why We Process Your Data

We only use your data where we have a legal basis:

  • Contract: To run the game, save your scores, and provide support.
  • Legal Obligation: To maintain financial records for tax purposes or respond to legal data requests.
  • Legitimate Interests: To improve game performance and ensure security (fraud prevention).

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share data only with these essential service providers:

  • Microsoft PlayFab: For backend account and leaderboard management.
  • Unity Ads: To deliver video advertisements. They may use your IP address and device IDs to provide relevant ads.
  • Google Play Services: For secure payment processing and account authentication.

5. International Transfers

Because our partners (Microsoft and Google) use global servers, your data may be stored outside the UK, primarily in the USA. We ensure these transfers are protected by standard contractual clauses approved by the UK Government.

6. Children’s Privacy

Captain's Haul is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we discover such data has been collected, we will delete it immediately.

7. Your Rights & Deletion

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your data. We provide a dedicated tool for these requests:

Click Here to Access or Delete Your Data

We will respond to all requests within 30 days. Note that deletion is permanent and will erase all scores and virtual currency.

8. Data Retention

  • Financial Records: Retained for 6 years as required by UK HMRC tax law.
  • Game Data: Accounts inactive for 24 months may be deleted.
  • Audit Trail: Records of data requests are kept for 2 years to prove legal compliance.
