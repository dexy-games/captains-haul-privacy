2. Information We Collect

We collect limited data to provide gameplay functionality and support our leaderboard system:

  • Identifiers: A unique Player ID generated by the App and your chosen Display Name for the leaderboards.
  • Device Information: Your device model, operating system version, and IP address (collected automatically by our backend for security and sync purposes).
  • Gameplay Data: High scores, levels reached, and virtual item balances (Doubloons).
  • Advertising IDs: Your mobile advertising identifier (AAID on Android) is used by our advertising partners to serve ads.

3. Third-Party Data Processing

The App uses professional third-party services that collect data to keep the game running:

  • Microsoft PlayFab: Used to store your high scores and "Hall of Champions" leaderboard data.
  • Unity Ads: Used to provide interstitial and rewarded advertisements. Unity may collect data for personalized and non-personalized advertising.
  • Google Play Services: Handles in-app purchases and basic app telemetry.

4. Children’s Privacy

Captain's Haul is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 years of age.

If you are a parent and believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child under 13, contact us at privacymatters@dexygames.com and we will delete the information within 30 days.

We comply with the UK Children’s Code by ensuring high-privacy settings are the default for our gameplay features.

5. Lawful Basis for Processing

Under the UK GDPR, we process your data based on:

  • Contractual Necessity: To provide the game service and leaderboards you requested.
  • Legitimate Interests: To prevent cheating, improve app performance, and show ads that keep the game free to play.
  • Consent: For personalized advertising (which you can manage via your device settings).

6. Your Legal Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of the data we hold on you.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request that we delete your leaderboard scores and Player ID.
  • Right to Object: Opt-out of personalized ads via your Android/Google account settings.
  • Right to Complain: If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please contact us first. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

7. Data Retention & Security

Retention: We keep leaderboard data as long as the "Hall of Champions" remains active. If you are inactive for more than 24 months, we may delete your data.

Security: We use industry-standard encryption (SSL/TLS) for data in transit between the App and PlayFab.
